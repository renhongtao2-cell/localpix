Free Background Remover — No Upload, No Watermark

Most "free" background removers upload your photo to a server, slap a watermark on the result, or downgrade you to a tiny thumbnail. LocalPix is different: it removes backgrounds entirely inside your browser, leaves no watermark, and exports at full resolution — unlimited times, for free.

How a no-upload background remover works

Instead of sending your image to a cloud server for processing, LocalPix loads the photo directly into your browser's memory and runs the cutout on your device's CPU/GPU. The image is never transmitted anywhere. That single design choice is why there's no upload step, no server queue, and no privacy fine print.

  • Open the tool — your file is read locally the moment you drop it in.
  • Cutout runs on-device — background pixels are detected and made transparent in real time.
  • Download instantly — you get a transparent PNG straight from your browser. No server ever saw it.

Why "no upload" actually matters

If you edit product photos for a store, or client images for a design job, uploading them to a random SaaS means a copy of that file now lives on someone else's disk — and in some services, gets used to train AI. A local-first tool removes that risk completely. For solo sellers and freelancers who handle confidential or pre-launch visuals, "never leaves the device" isn't a feature, it's a requirement.

Free, full-resolution, unlimited

Plenty of tools show you a clean preview, then hand you a 625×400 pixel download unless you pay. LocalPix exports at the original resolution on the free plan — no credit meter, no expiry, no "upgrade to unlock HD" wall. You can process one image or one thousand; the price stays $0.
