Why sellers want a private editor

Pre-launch products and client work are confidential. Uploading them to a random SaaS means copies sit on someone else's servers — and may train their AI. A local-first editor keeps every original on your machine. For solo sellers handling unreleased inventory, "never leaves the device" is the difference between safe and exposed.
