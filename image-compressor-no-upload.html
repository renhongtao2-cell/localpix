Private Image Compressor — Shrink Files Without Uploading

Compressing a photo usually means uploading it to a website, waiting in a queue, then downloading the result. LocalPix compresses JPG, PNG and WebP images right in your browser — the file is never sent anywhere, and you keep full control of quality.

Why compress images privately?

Smaller images load faster, which matters for product listings, store pages, and email attachments. But most online compressors require you to hand over the file first. If the image is a client deliverable, an unreleased product, or anything confidential, that upload is a leak waiting to happen. A private compressor keeps the whole job on your device.

How the in-browser compressor works

  • Drop in your image — it's read locally and never leaves the tab.
  • Set quality — a simple slider trades file size for sharpness (80% is a good default).
  • Download — get the smaller file instantly, straight from your browser.

Because there's no server round-trip, even large batches feel instant. No waiting for "processing" spinners.

Keep the quality you control

LocalPix doesn't decide the quality for you behind a paywall. You pick the level, see the resulting size, and download. Nothing is silently capped or watermarked. Free, unlimited, no account required.
